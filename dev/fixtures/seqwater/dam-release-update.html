Flood releases commenced from North Pine Dam – 11:00pm, 22/10/2026

AFFECTED - North Pine (Lake Samsonvale)

LAST UPDATED - 25/10/2026 - 06:27

Home Dam release update

Dam release update

Latest update: 6.00am, Sunday 25 October 2026

Seqwater Flood Operations Centre remains at Stand Up with controlled, minor flood releases from North Pine Dam underway since 11pm on 22 October 2026 due to continuous rainfall and inflows.

GATED DAMS:

North Pine Dam - Flood releases began at 11pm on Thursday, 22 October 2026 and will continue today.

Youngs Crossing Road is expected to reopen today.

If you are downstream of the dam, stay away from fast flowing or deep water near waterways and floodplains.

Somerset Dam - Operational releases from Somerset into Wivenhoe Dam began at 1pm on Thursday, 22 October 2026. 

These operational releases are expected to continue over the next several days.

Wivenhoe Dam - Flood releases from Wivenhoe Dam are not expected.

UNGATED DAMS: 

Due to heavy rainfall, the following ungated dams are spilling excess water:

  • Cooloolabin Dam
  • Poona Dam
  • Wyaralong Dam.

Due to continuing rain in the catchment area, outflows have increased from the following dams:

  • Baroon Pocket Dam
  • Borumba Dam
  • Cedar Pocket Dam
  • Enoggera Dam
  • Ewen Maddock Dam
  • Gold Creek Dam
  • Lake Macdonald Dam
  • Little Nerang Dam
  • Maroon Dam
  • Sideling Creek Dam
  • Wappa Dam.

Avoid potential hazards downstream of the dam such as fast flowing or deep water near waterways and floodplains.

 

Dam release notification service

Seqwater provides a free dam release notification service that provides subscribers with notifications by email, text messages to mobile phones or recorded messages to telephone landlines when dam releases are occurring.
