    Choose a gauge on the map to see its readings and last 3 days.

    Notifications

    Plus alerts for gauges on your watch list (open a gauge and tap ☆ Watch) and for your areas below.

    Your areas

      Zoom the map to the area you care about (a single gauge, a town, a stretch of river), name it and add it. You'll be told about gauge alerts and BoM warnings inside it.

      Flood maps

      Shading shows where flooding has happened or could happen, not current flooding or depth. Hover over the map, or tap it on a phone, to see what applies at that spot. The state floodplain map is a rough guide drawn from land shape, not a flood model.

      Check your council's flood maps for detail, and the state's FloodCheck.

      Detailed council flood maps: Brisbane, Lockyer Valley, Somerset and the state's FloodCheck.
